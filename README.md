# Machine Learning Gymnasium

This repository serves as a space to archive a mixture of implementations, challenges, puzzles, experiments, and computational studies exploring mathematical foundations and empirical behaviour.

The material varies considerably in scope, from quick puzzles and daily problem-solving challenges to structured experiments and more comprehensive investigations. Some entries are exercises in implementing and understanding established methods; others venture into their mathematical properties, numerical behaviour, or practical performance. Above all, this is an evolving archive of computational curiosities, intellectual workouts, and interesting problems that don't quite belong anywhere else.

## Organisation

- **Studies:** More comprehensive investigations involving mathematical derivations, implementations, experiments, and analysis.
- **Sprints:** Focused, structured exercises with a defined objective, typically completed over a relatively short period.
- **Challenges:** Programming and ML problems undertaken to practise particular techniques or develop problem-solving skills.
- **Puzzles:** Mathematical, statistical, or conceptual problems explored primarily out of curiosity.
